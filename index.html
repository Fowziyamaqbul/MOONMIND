THE WHY

Your brain does a lot.
Let it clock out.

Sleep supports learning, mood, attention and everyday recovery. A perfect routine isn’t the goal. A repeatable one is.

FRIENDLY REMINDERS

Make room for
tomorrow-you.

Choose habits that fit your life and let them evolve. No perfection test.

01 / WIND DOWN
☼

Lower the volume.

Try a calm activity before bed: a shower, a few pages, gentle music or slow breathing.

02 / FIND A RHYTHM
◷

Keep time kindly.

A fairly consistent sleep and wake time can help your body recognise its rhythm.

03 / MAKE IT COMFY
☁

Set the scene.

When possible, make your room quiet, dim and comfortable. Start with one small change.
